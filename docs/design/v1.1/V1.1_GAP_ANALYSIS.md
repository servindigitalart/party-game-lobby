## 5. Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recolouring the loop breaks Firestore sync | **Very low** | High | Colour work touches only `build`; `AGENTS.md` mandates sync-first. Keep colour PRs separate from any timing change. |
| Deleting legacy tokens reskins unmigrated screens silently | Medium | High | Deprecate rather than delete; migrate call sites; `flutter analyze` gives a live burndown |
| No golden tests ⇒ visual regressions land unnoticed | **High** | Medium | Fase 3B's own criterion. Add goldens for the 6 core components **before** the 3E–3G recolour, not after |

## 6. Recommended 1.1 scope boundary

**INFERENCE.** This audit surfaced ~70 actionable items. Shipping all of them is not a release, it is a
rewrite. The defensible boundary for a visual/UX release:

**IN — 1.1 ships:**
1. Brand at the OS level: icon, launch screen, app name, bundled fonts, isotype in-app.
2. The navigation shell (reachability of profile + leaderboard).
3. Fase 3E/3F/3G: recolour and re-hierarchy the three live-game screens and the winner.
4. The Quadrant-1 list in full (14 items, all under ~3 h each).
5. The accessibility package.
6. Five component primitives + the two P0 component fixes (`AnimatedPrimaryButton`, `GameCard`).
7. Copy centralisation into `GameCopy`, eliminating all 6 raw-exception strings.
8. Golden tests for the 6 core components, added **before** the recolour.

**OUT — deferred to 1.2 with a named trigger:**
Audio identity · Rive · `flutter_animate` · pack UI (until content expands) · onboarding · settings
beyond a mute toggle · custom icon font and brand avatars **if** design capacity is unavailable ·
Fase 3H/3I (profile and leaderboard recolour — reachability first, beauty second) · the pre-reveal
silence · QR join · night recap.

**INFERENCE — the sequencing that matters most.** Make the meta layer *reachable* (P0) before making
it *beautiful* (3H/3I). An ugly screen a player can reach is worth more than a beautiful one they
cannot. This inverts the design system's own Fase ordering, and it is the right inversion: Cap. 35 was
written before the reachability gap was known.
